# Data notice

The initial public release contains no WSJ0 recordings, mixtures, model-output
audio, or spectrograms derived from those recordings. The reported scores are
aggregate research results. WSJ0 publication permissions remain to be confirmed
against the research group's applicable agreement.

The [official WSJ0 catalog](https://catalog.ldc.upenn.edu/LDC93S6A) links the
[LDC non-member agreement](https://catalog.ldc.upenn.edu/license/ldc-non-members-agreement.pdf),
which permits limited excerpts in documents describing non-commercial research.
The agreement does not state a universal number of clips or seconds. Membership
and corpus-specific terms can differ; that generic clause alone does not establish
permission for this project's proposed public audio release.

Future audio will retain its source terms and attribution, separate from any code
license. No license to the original WSJ0 recordings is granted by this repository.
