Playback notes

Eight randomly selected mixtures, with both target queries retained. Playback uses the first up to 6 seconds of each signal and up to 3 seconds of a separate enrollment utterance. Outputs are precomputed from full-utterance inference.

Mixture, clean targets and extracted outputs share one gain to prevent clipping. Their players share the playback position; enrollment is independent. Spectrograms show 0–4 kHz from bottom to top with a common −80 to −20 dB magnitude scale relative to amplitude 1.
